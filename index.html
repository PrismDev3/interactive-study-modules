Fan Project Notice

Unofficial · Non-Commercial
Personal Educational Homage · Non-Commercial · Free to Play

This is an unofficial, non-profit fan-made game. Developed strictly as a personal, educational hobby project to practice 3D web game mechanics, custom physics, procedural lighting, and interactive brick rendering. It is not an official product and is strictly not for sale.

No Affiliation

This fan project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to The LEGO Group, Marvel Entertainment, The Walt Disney Company, Sony Pictures, Insomniac Games, or any of their subsidiaries or affiliates. All characters, names, logos, suits, and trademarks belong exclusively to their respective owners.

Non-Commercial & 100% Free

This project is completely free to experience. No money is charged now or ever:

  • Zero Cost: No purchase price, no subscriptions, no ads, and no donations.
  • No Microtransactions: No real-money store, no crypto, no NFTs, and no resale.
  • Cosmetic Only: All studs and suit unlocks exist solely in your local browser save.

Fan-Made Assets & Fair Use

All minifigure models, textures, animations, city geometry, shaders, and web audio are fan-made recreations, procedural art, or low-detail homages created specifically for this project. None are extracted from commercial titles. Created under fair use for educational commentary.

Takedown Policy & Safety

Takedown Policy: If you own rights to any intellectual property represented here and object to its inclusion, please contact the author and it will be removed promptly with zero dispute. This hobby project honors all rights holders' wishes.

Audience & Safety: Intended for players aged 13+. Multiplayer rooms are player-hosted and unmoderated. Play responsibly and never share private personal information in chat.
