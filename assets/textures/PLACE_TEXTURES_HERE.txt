Drop your PBR texture maps here with these exact filenames:

  albedo.png     — Base color / diffuse (sRGB)
  normal.png     — Tangent-space normal map (Linear)
  roughness.png  — Roughness in R channel (Linear)
  metallic.png   — Metalness in R channel (Linear)

If you use a combined ORM (Occlusion-Roughness-Metalness) texture,
split the channels or update the material in src/SuitMaterial.js.

For the hero suit specifically:
  - Red/blue base color drives most of the look from albedo.png
  - roughness ~0.5 on the fabric, ~0.2 on the lenses
  - metalness ~0.0 on fabric, ~0.3 on web-shooters
  - The hex web pattern is procedurally generated on top of your albedo
    so a plain red/blue texture is enough to get the look working.
